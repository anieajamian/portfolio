About

Anie Ajamian

Hi, I'm Anie — a product designer in Los Angeles. My favorite kind of design is the kind you don't really notice, because it just works. If something feels calm, obvious, and easy to move through, that usually means a lot of careful decisions went in that you'll never see. That's the part I love.

I started out in graphic and web design, making brand and marketing sites, before I found my way to product. What really changed how I work were the years spent inside early- and mid-stage startups, elbow to elbow with founders, PMs, and engineers. I stopped thinking about the one perfect screen and started caring about how the whole thing holds together when reality gets in the way: legacy systems, franchise owners, compliance, a funding runway that won't wait.

These days I work across product, brand, and systems, usually somewhere fast-moving and a little ambiguous. Give me a messy, tangled problem and my instinct is to keep pulling threads until it turns into something clear, usable, and actually human. That's the work I'm happiest doing.

When there's no messy problem on my plate, I go find one. The FitOn and Supper Club case studies in my work are self-directed projects I took on just to keep sharp and chase ideas no one's paying me to solve yet.

Outside of work, I'm usually traveling, poking around new places and cultures, or spending time with family. I'm also a little obsessed with interiors and well-designed spaces. Product, hotel, or my own living room, it's always the small details that get me.
