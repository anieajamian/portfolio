About

Anie Ajamian

Hi, I'm Anie. I'm a product designer in Los Angeles, and most of my career has been spent working on the everyday stuff people have to figure out, like refilling a prescription, paying a bill, booking a hotel room, and checking out. Things that sound simple until you actually try to use them. I like figuring out why they're complicated in the first place, and then making them simpler.

I started my career in graphic and web design before moving into product, and I've worked with everything from early-stage startups to much larger products. Working closely with founders, PMs, and engineers taught me that good product design is about more than the screen in front of you. I like getting involved early, figuring out what we're actually trying to solve, and working through the messy parts with the team.

I care about the big picture, but I'm also a designer who likes to get into the weeds. I want to understand the problem, talk to users, look at the data, test things, and obsess over the details that make an experience feel right. Sometimes the answer is a new flow. Sometimes it's a much smaller change. Sometimes it's realizing we're solving the wrong problem entirely.

Over the course of my career, I've learned there usually isn't one perfect answer. A lot of the job is asking good questions, making tradeoffs, changing your mind when you need to, and knowing what's worth spending time on. I also really value working with people who ask for help, challenge each other, share what they know, and don't take themselves too seriously.

Outside of work, life is pretty full. I have kids and two dogs, so our weekends usually involve the beach, parks, museums, or wherever we can get outside. I love to travel, partly because I like being somewhere unfamiliar and seeing how other people live. I also can't seem to turn off the part of my brain that notices how things work, so I'm usually paying attention to a confusing sign, a weird checkout flow, or why someone designed something the way they did.
